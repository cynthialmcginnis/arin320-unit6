How to play

  1. Read the clue. It is the definition of a term from Units 0 to 6.
  2. The player whose name is outlined in orange guesses one letter. Select it on the screen or type it on the keyboard.
  3. A correct letter shows everywhere it appears in the term, and the same player guesses again.
  4. A wrong letter knocks one part off the robot, and the turn passes to the other player.
  5. The player who finishes the term scores 10 points plus 1 point for each part the robot still has.
  6. If the robot loses all 7 parts, it powers down and nobody scores.
  7. When a term ends, the player who did not score reads the definition out loud.
  8. The game ends when you finish every term or the clock runs out. Then print or save your score.
